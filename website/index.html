Capabilities

Why Publix

Privacy first

Results go only to your inbox — never displayed on screen. Submitting an email proves you own it, preventing unauthorised lookups on other people's addresses.

Multiple breach sources

We aggregate across XposedOrNot, LeakCheck, HIBP Pro, and Dehashed — covering billions of records from hundreds of known breach datasets.

Encrypted storage

All data at rest is protected with AES-256-GCM encryption. No plaintext passwords are ever stored. Your email is hashed before any external query.

Free + Pro tiers

The basic breach check is free — covering the most common sources. Upgrade to Pro for full stealer-log intelligence, detailed password exposure data, and priority sources.
